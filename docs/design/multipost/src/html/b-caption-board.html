B · Caption, evolved

The app you have now, given a brand. The post is a caption: white subtitle boxes for the shared caption and cyan for a destination's own. The new mark is two subtitle lines, one white and one cyan, which is the app's core idea in a 48 dp tile.

What changes

  • A launcher mark and wordmark lockup that state the shared/own idea.
  • The library header carries the lockup; what needs you is subtitled at the top.
  • States become broadcast bugs: a black box with a coloured icon, a word and a time.
  • “Write once. Share your way.” set as subtitles on first run.

What it keeps

  • The current tokens, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, the true-black stage and the letterboxing.
  • Cyan for own captions, white for shared captions and the main action.
  • Every native screen, the evidence and the tests largely as they are.

Main risk

It's the smallest change and may still read as “Caption”. True black brings OLED smear when scrolling. Subtitles over bright photos still rely on their boxes.
