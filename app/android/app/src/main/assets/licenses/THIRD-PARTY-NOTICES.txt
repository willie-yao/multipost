Caption includes the unmodified official Twitter Text Java library 3.1.0
(com.twitter.twittertext:twitter-text) and its jsr305 2.0.1 annotation dependency
(com.google.code.findbugs:jsr305). Both published POMs declare Apache License 2.0.

Twitter Text: Copyright 2011 Twitter, Inc.
Source: https://github.com/twitter/twitter-text

The library is called through an X-only native channel. Caption does not copy
or modify its Unicode, emoji, or URL parsing algorithm.
